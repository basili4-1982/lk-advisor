# Public Catalog Patterns in B2B Software Distribution and Marketplaces

Scope note: research conducted Nov 2026 against public catalog pages, distributor help/API documentation and vendor licensing pages. No private account data used. Where a source could not be retrieved (Insight Enterprises catalog and product pages repeatedly timed out; Ingram Micro's own Xvantage/cloud marketplace pages timed out or returned 403; Kaspersky's legacy business tiering page 404'd), this is recorded as a gap rather than filled from memory. Ingram Micro was covered instead through a third-party integration vendor's API documentation, which is flagged as such. The МагПро/Криптоком vendor site was reachable but served in Windows-1251; its January 2026 price list was read directly as a PDF, so those findings are primary.

---

## How do SoftwareONE, SHI, Insight, CDW, Ingram Micro, Pax8 and similar distributors structure software families, editions, license terms, renewals, upgrades and alternatives in their public catalogs?

### Takeaway
The mature distributors all converge on a four-level data model — **vendor → product (family) → item/SKU (edition × term × quantity tier × delivery) → listing/offer (price, market, eligibility)** — with license *terms* modelled as structured attributes on the sellable item rather than as free text. Renewals and upgrades are separate SKUs, not a property of the base SKU, and they are surfaced as lifecycle actions on the subscription rather than as catalog siblings.

### Cited Findings

**SoftwareOne — explicit, documented object model (the most useful public reference)**
- SoftwareOne's Marketplace Platform defines five actors: **Vendors** (develop and sell products), **Distributors** (resell vendor solutions to resellers), **Associates** (internal), **Partners** (buy to resell), **Clients** (buy for own consumption) — [SoftwareOne docs, Key concepts](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/marketplace-platform/platform-overview/key-concepts.md).
- The `Product` object is defined as "a collection of items and their relevant parameters curated into a cohesive group for business purposes. Defined by vendors, products are accessible through listings" — i.e. the product is the *family*, and it is not itself transactable — [SoftwareOne Catalog API, Product](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/product.md).
- The `Item` object is "a 'product item' as a transactable element of a product that ... is represented as a 'line item' of that order", carrying `terms`, `quantityNotApplicable`, `parameters` (with `externalId` used for the vendor SKU), `group` (itemGroup) and `unit` (unit of measure) — [SoftwareOne Catalog API, Item](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/items.md).
- License terms are a structured object on the item, exemplified as `{ "period": "1m", "commitment": "1y" }` — billing period and commitment are modelled **separately**, which is exactly the distinction Microsoft NCE and Adobe VIP need — [SoftwareOne Catalog API, Item](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/items.md).
- `ItemGroup` is the selection-step primitive inside a purchase wizard: fields `name`, `label` ("displayed in the wizard steps selector"), `description`, `displayOrder`, `default`, `multiple`, `required`, `itemCount`. Real example: group "Items" / label "Select items" / `required: true`, containing M365 Online Services plus a bundled SoftwareOne service — [SoftwareOne Catalog API, Item Group](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/item-group.md).
- `Listing` decouples *what* is sold from *who sells it and at what price*: "a business entity that aligns a product with a specific SoftwareOne seller. It's defined by an authorization and an assigned price list"; it carries `primary` (which listing the purchase wizard uses when a product has several from the same seller), `eligibility.client` / `eligibility.partner`, and `statistics.subscriptions` / `statistics.agreements` — [SoftwareOne Catalog API, Listing](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/listing.md).
- Product lifecycle states are `Draft`, `Pending`, `Published`, `Unpublished`; items use `Draft`, `Published`, `Unpublished` — [SoftwareOne Catalog API, Product](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/product.md); [Item](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/items.md).
- Products can carry `Document` objects (name, type, language, url) — used for datasheets/licensing guides attached at product level, not SKU level — [SoftwareOne Catalog API, Document](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/documentation.md).
- Product-level `settings` include vendor-specific validation hooks for "item selection" compatibility in the purchase wizard — i.e. the platform enforces *which items may be combined*, not just which are related — [SoftwareOne Catalog API, Product](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/product.md).
- Public storefront navigation is three-axis: by **Vendor**, by **Product**, by **Category** (`/vendor`, `/product`, `/category`) — [SoftwareOne Marketplace](https://platform.softwareone.com/product).
- SoftwareOne markets "search by almost 80 different software categories" in the client portal, with top categories listed as business applications, collaboration & productivity, graphics & design, security, virtualization, backup & recovery — [SoftwareOne, Explore by category](https://www.softwareone.com/en-us/marketplace/explore-by-category).
- Category pages are merchandised with a definition paragraph plus a product grid (e.g. "Database Software ... designed to store, organize, and retrieve information") — [SoftwareOne category example](https://platform.softwareone.com/category/database-software/CAT-0027-6837).

**Renewals and upgrades as lifecycle objects (SoftwareOne + Microsoft)**
- SoftwareOne exposes exactly three renewal outcomes for CSP subscriptions: **renew to a regular term**, **renew to an Extended Service Term (EST)**, or **cancel at expiration** — [SoftwareOne docs, renewal options](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/extensions/microsoft-cloud-solution-provider/additional-resources/faqs/what-subscription-renewal-options-are-available.md).
- EST converts the subscription to a monthly term that runs until cancelled; once converted, **no changes can be made**; EST bills monthly at the monthly rate **+3% uplift (or +23% if no monthly plan exists)**; trial subscriptions and end-of-sale SKUs are ineligible — [same](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/extensions/microsoft-cloud-solution-provider/additional-resources/faqs/what-subscription-renewal-options-are-available.md).
- Adobe VIP Marketplace in SoftwareOne: a **termination order** returns a whole subscription only if *every* license is within the **14-day** return window and after the most recent renewal; otherwise the correct action is to turn off auto-renewal, or to **reduce quantity** to return only part — [SoftwareOne docs, Terminate and return Adobe licenses](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/extensions/adobe-vip-marketplace/returns-and-termination/terminate-and-return-adobe-licenses.md).
- Microsoft models upgrade as a **Step-Up (SU)** license type in volume licensing: the order table column "Step-up* — Indicates if the product or service can be 'stepped up' to higher editions. Select Plus only"; the License summary page counts "SA Step-Up" alongside L&SA and SA in "Active SA quantity" — [Microsoft Learn, View VL contracts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/commerce/licenses/view-vl-contracts?view=o365-worldwide).
- Microsoft explicitly documents that **Step-Up licenses are not covered by the Extended Period Term**, with the canonical example "a customer who has Microsoft 365 E3 steps up to Microsoft 365 E5" — [Microsoft Learn, OLS lifecycle and EPT](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/volume-licensing-central/learning/contracting/ols-lifecycle-and-ept).
- Microsoft continues to add **step-up SKUs** as a productized motion: Jan 2026 partner announcement covers "new CSP step up SKUs for Entra ID P2 and Defender for Endpoint P2" and an "incremental pricing model" — [Microsoft Learn, January 2026 announcements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-january).
- Microsoft also ships a *migration* relation distinct from upgrade: "Protection against capability loss when transitioning customers from EA to CSP" — [same](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-january).

**SHI — spec-sheet cataloguing, normalization by attribute, not by hierarchy**
- SHI product pages are titled `"<Product> | Overview, Specs, Details"` and split into **Overview** (marketing prose + "Additional Details") and **Product Specs** (attribute table) — [SHI product page example](https://www.shi.com/product/48038154/Beyond-Compare-5-Pro-Edition-per-seat-100-Users).
- SHI's product *titles* are generated from a fixed attribute order, not written by hand: `Beyond Compare Pro - (v. 5) - license - 1 seat - volume - 10-49 licenses - ESD - Linux`; `Citrix SD-WAN 210-100 Standard Edition - On-Premise subscription license (1 year) - Easy License`; `SECRET SERVER - PRO EDITION - TIER 3 (100-249 USERS) - MAINT.` — [SHI](https://www.shi.com/product/48038154/Beyond-Compare-5-Pro-Edition-per-seat-100-Users).
- The corresponding spec fields are separately faceted: `Product Type`, `Software / License Type`, `Software / Licensing Program`, `Software / Volume Pricing Level`, `License Pricing`, `License Qty`, `Details` (e.g. ESD) — same page.
- SHI's title pattern therefore encodes six independent dimensions that any B2B software catalog must keep separable: **product, version, license model, quantity+unit, volume tier, delivery format, platform** — [SHI](https://www.shi.com/product/48038154/Beyond-Compare-5-Pro-Edition-per-seat-100-Users) (inference from the observed naming grammar).
- SHI hosts vendor-supplied **edition comparison matrices** as brand-page microsites, e.g. Adobe Acrobat DC compared across Standard/Pro × Perpetual/Subscription with feature rows grouped into sections ("Interact with PDFs", "Create PDF files", "Edit PDFs", "Sign and collect", "Deploy and manage across the organization") — [SHI Acrobat DC comparison](https://www.content.shi.com/SHIcom/ContentAttachmentFiles/Adobe070915BrandPage/adc-comparison.html).

**CDW — facet-heavy taxonomy + license-type facets + bundle module**
- CDW's software taxonomy is a 3-level browse tree: `Software → Business Applications → {Application Suites, CRM, Digital Signage, Document Management, Point of Sale, Presentations, Reporting & Data Analytics, Project Management, Spreadsheets, Word Processing, Voice Recognition}` — [CDW Business Applications](https://www.cdw.com/category/software/business-applications/?w=FE).
- CDW exposes **license type as a facet**, with values including `Site License`, `Software assurance`, `Step-up`, `Subscription License Renewal`, `Subscription Plan License Key`, `Subscription Upgrade License`, `Product upgrade license`, `Professional License`, `Site License (renewal)`, `Software assurance (upgrade)` — [CDW category facets](https://www.cdw.com/category/software/?w=F); [CDW.ca facet list](https://www.cdw.ca/category/software/?w=F).
- CDW PDPs carry a compact "Software Details" chip list that is effectively the variant key: `365 A3 / 1 user / academic / All Languages / Subscription license / hosted / EES`, plus `Mfg # AAD-38391-12mo` and internal `CDW # 5419407` — [CDW PDP, Microsoft 365 A3](https://www.cdw.com/product/microsoft-365-a3-subscription-license-1-user/5419407).
- CDW PDPs include **Add to Compare**, **Save to Favorites**, and an **"Enhance your purchase" → "Better Together"** module that shows "Current Item", running "Total Price" and a single Add to Cart for the bundle — [same](https://www.cdw.com/product/microsoft-365-a3-subscription-license-1-user/5419407).
- CDW's product titles use the same attribute grammar as SHI (`<Vendor> <Product> <Edition> - <license model> - <qty> <unit>`), e.g. "Google Workspace Enterprise Plus - subscription license (1 year) - 1 user", "Microsoft Office Professional Plus - software assurance - 1 device", "Snagit - Business subscription license (1 year) - 1 user" — [CDW software category](https://www.cdw.com/category/software/?w=F).

**Pax8 / Ingram Micro / TD SYNNEX — how the *product API* is shaped (third-party integration docs)**

Integration documentation from Neo (an MSP automation vendor) gives an independent, implementation-level view of how these marketplaces actually expose products, which is more concrete than their marketing pages:
- Pax8's `Products & Pricing` permission group covers "Products, your cost per billing term, **commitment terms** and **provisioning details** (read-only)" — so the sellable unit is (product × billing term × commitment) plus a provisioning payload — [Neo docs, Pax8](https://docs.neoagent.io/integrations/marketplaces/pax8.md).
- Ingram Micro Cloud Marketplace's equivalent group covers "Products, prices, **plans** and **activation parameters** (read-only)" — "plans" is a distinct level below product, and orders require activation parameters, i.e. the SKU is not fully described until configured — [Neo docs, Ingram Micro](https://docs.neoagent.io/integrations/marketplaces/ingram-micro.md).
- TD SYNNEX StreamOne ION: "look up products, **SKUs, plans** and customer prices", and subscriptions carry "seat counts, renewal dates and auto-renew settings" — [Neo docs, TD SYNNEX ION](https://docs.neoagent.io/integrations/marketplaces/td-synnex.md).
- Across all three, **subscriptions** (not orders) are the durable object: seat counts, term, renewal date, auto-renew flag, and a subscription ID that must be targeted explicitly because one customer may hold several subscriptions for the same licence (documented example: an "80/20 yearly/monthly split" of the same Microsoft licence) — [Neo docs, Marketplaces overview](https://docs.neoagent.io/integrations/marketplaces/intro.md).
- Lifecycle operations exposed per marketplace are consistently: read products/prices, price an order without placing it ("validation-only order"), order a product the client does not hold, **renew** a subscription, change seats, cancel — [Neo docs, Ingram Micro](https://docs.neoagent.io/integrations/marketplaces/ingram-micro.md); [Pax8](https://docs.neoagent.io/integrations/marketplaces/pax8.md).

**Pax8 — packaged solutions and multi-product carts instead of SKU-level relations**
- Pax8's Catalog is positioned on **compare + multi-product cart**: "Compare products based on client requirements", "Build multi-product carts that increase deal size", "Filter and compare options without manual research", "granular categorization", "smart search, advanced sorting" — [Pax8 Marketplace tools](https://www.pax8.com/en-us/marketplace/tools/); [Pax8 blog, new Marketplace](https://www.pax8.com/blog/new-pax8-marketplace/).
- **Solutions Builder** is the bundle primitive: "Package multi-product offerings once, then sell them across your entire base", "Standardize your top sellers for repeatable revenue, not one-off builds", "Combine products and solutions into sellable services" — [Pax8 Marketplace tools](https://www.pax8.com/en-us/marketplace/tools/).
- **Custom Products** lets a partner sell its own services "alongside vendor SKUs", with the stated goal of "a single source of truth for all your offerings", "standardize and optimize product listings", "align product, pricing and billing data" — [same](https://www.pax8.com/en-us/marketplace/tools/).
- Pax8 also ships **custom Storefronts** and **Quotes** as downstream surfaces of the same catalog — [Pax8 blog](https://www.pax8.com/blog/new-pax8-marketplace/).

### Inferences
- A workable minimum viable model is: `Vendor → Product(family) → Item(SKU) → Listing(offer)`, with `terms{period, commitment}` and `parameters{SKU}` on the item. SoftwareOne's public API is strong evidence this is sufficient for multi-currency, multi-market, partner-vs-client selling.
- Because SHI and CDW both *generate* titles from attributes, a catalog should store attributes and render titles, never store a title as the source of truth. This is what makes "one family, many SKUs" navigable.
- Renewal and upgrade must be **typed relations between SKUs** (renewal-of, upgrade-to/step-up-from, migration-from) plus **lifecycle state on the subscription**; treating them as plain "related products" loses the constraint that an upgrade must match the installed edition and term.

### Gaps
- Insight Enterprises' public product/catalog pages could not be retrieved (repeated timeouts, no cached text); no citable evidence gathered on Insight's taxonomy or PDP modules. Insight is therefore absent from this research rather than characterised from memory.
- Ingram Micro's own Xvantage and cloud-marketplace pages returned 403 or timed out. Its product model is described only via a third-party integrator's API documentation (product → plan → activation parameters), not from Ingram's primary documentation.
- No public source found describing SoftwareOne's *editorial* process for assigning products to categories (manual vs automated).
- No public source explains how any distributor assigns a product to a *family* when the vendor does not supply that grouping (e.g. which SKUs belong to "Kaspersky Next"), so family-construction rules remain undocumented.

## How do they present "compare editions", "related products", "accessories", "alternatives", "renewals", "bundles"?

### Takeaway
Public catalogs use **six distinct presentation patterns**, and mature ones do not blur them: (1) a feature matrix across editions on a marketing page, (2) a side-by-side "compare" tray on the PDP, (3) a "better together"/"enhance your purchase" bundle block, (4) "this product is also supplied as part of kits" bundle-membership links, (5) an explicit migration/switch promotion, (6) a renewal action on the subscription record. "Related products" as a generic carousel is largely a B2C/e-commerce pattern; B2B distributors replace it with **comparison + configuration + solution packages**.

### Cited Findings
- **Compare editions as a feature matrix with grouped rows and orthogonal variants.** SHI/Adobe's Acrobat matrix columns are `Standard Perpetual | Standard Subscription | Pro Perpetual | Pro Subscription` — i.e. edition × commercial model are two separate axes, not one — [SHI Acrobat DC comparison](https://www.content.shi.com/SHIcom/ContentAttachmentFiles/Adobe070915BrandPage/adc-comparison.html).
- **Compare as a PDP action with a persistent tray.** CDW PDPs carry "Add to Compare" (alongside "Save to Favorites") — [CDW PDP](https://www.cdw.com/product/microsoft-365-a3-subscription-license-1-user/5419407). Softline's storefront runs the same pattern in Russian, with a header compare counter ("Сравнение") and a "Товар добавлен к сравнению" confirmation — [Softline store PDP](https://store.softline.ru/cryptocom/-525362/).
- **Bundle block on PDP with running total.** CDW "Enhance your purchase" → "Better Together" lists "Current Item" plus companions, shows "Total Price", single "Add to Cart" — [CDW PDP](https://www.cdw.com/product/microsoft-365-a3-subscription-license-1-user/5419407).
- **Bundle membership expressed from the component side.** Dr.Web product pages carry "Этот продукт также поставляется в составе комплектов" with links to *Комплект для малого бизнеса (5-50 ПК)*, *Комплект Для школ (5-500 ПК)*, *Dr.Web «Малый бизнес» (5 ПК и 1 сервер)* — [Dr.Web, лицензирование для бизнеса](https://license.drweb.ru/products/biz/).
- **Accessories/add-on components as a matrix, not a carousel.** Dr.Web presents "Варианты лицензий" as a table `Продукты Dr.Web | Базовая лицензия | Дополнительные компоненты Dr.Web`, where the control centre is either "БЕСПЛАТНО" or "включен в лицензию" depending on the suite — [same](https://license.drweb.ru/products/biz/).
- **Alternatives expressed as a priced migration offer.** Dr.Web runs a "Мигрировать Со скидкой 50%" link beside every suite, pointing at a dedicated migration promotion page — a competitor-switch relation with an incentive attached — [Dr.Web](https://license.drweb.ru/products/biz/); [Dr.Web migration promo](https://promotions.drweb.ru/promo/migrate/v2/).
- **Renewals presented as a subscription action, not a product.** SoftwareOne's renewal flow is Configure → choose "Renew to regular term" / "Renew to Extended Service Term" / "Cancel at expiration" → order; the renewal *policy* is displayed on the subscription details and on the Microsoft accounts page — [SoftwareOne docs](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/extensions/microsoft-cloud-solution-provider/additional-resources/faqs/what-subscription-renewal-options-are-available.md).
- **Co-termination is the B2B renewal UX norm.** Adobe: "All team licenses will co-terminate and will automatically renew, on your annual renewal date, until you cancel"; seats added mid-term co-terminate with the account anniversary rather than starting their own 12-month clock — [Adobe subscription terms](https://www.adobe.com/legal/subscription-terms.html); [TechZoid, VIP vs VIP Marketplace](https://techzoidtechnologies.com/blog/adobe-vip-versus-vip-marketplace).
- **Upgrade as an explicit compare page, not an inference.** Adobe runs a dedicated "Compare your plan to Creative Cloud Pro for teams" page for the individual→teams upgrade, listing what the target adds (admin console, 1TB pooled storage, 180-day version history, asset reclaim, centralized deployment, 24x7 support, 1:1 expert sessions) — [Adobe, upgrade to teams](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/business/teams/upgradetoteams.html).
- **Generic e-commerce relation labels** that a B2B catalog would inherit if built on commodity commerce: "Customers Also Bought", "Recommended Accessories", "You May Also Need", "Related Products", "Up-Sells" — [CS-Cart recommendation extension](https://marketplace.cs-cart.com/advanced-product-recommendations.html); [BigCommerce cross-sell guide](https://www.bigcommerce.com/articles/ecommerce/upselling-and-cross-selling/).

### Inferences
- The B2B pattern is **"compare" (deliberate, attribute-based) over "recommend" (speculative, behavioural)**. Compare matrices are curated once per vendor family and reused across every SKU in that family; this is far cheaper to maintain than per-SKU relations and is what makes vendor families scale.
- "Accessories" is a poor fit for software. The functional equivalent is **components / add-ons / capacity packs** (e.g. a management console, an additional mailbox address licence, a TLS gateway module), and these should be modelled as items in an add-on group with `required`/`multiple` flags — mirroring SoftwareOne's ItemGroup semantics.
- The bundle block and the "also part of kits" block are the same relation traversed in opposite directions; one edge type (`bundle_contains`) with inverse rendering is enough.

### Gaps
- No public source found quantifying the conversion effect of "compare editions" modules in B2B software specifically (only general e-commerce merchandising claims, which are vendor marketing).
- Could not verify whether CDW's "Better Together" companions are hand-picked or rule-driven; no public documentation.
- No public evidence of "alternatives" being computed (e.g. same category + same license unit) by any of these distributors; only curated migration pages were observable.

## How do they handle vendor families (Microsoft 365, Adobe, Kaspersky, КриптоПро), product naming, SKU/edition normalization, and family/variant navigation?

### Takeaway
Every large vendor publishes a **plan/edition ladder with an explicit upgrade direction**, and each ladder has a different *axis of variation*: Microsoft varies by plan tier + add-on + commitment; Adobe by segment (individual/team/enterprise) + app bundle (single app vs all apps) + co-terminous seats; Kaspersky by protection tier (Foundations → Optimum → XDR → MXDR); КриптоПро by product line × version (4.0/5.0, R2/R3) × execution/platform (КС2/КС3, Astra Linux, TLS) × license type (base/extension/upgrade). A single generic "edition" field cannot hold these; the axis must be per-family.

### Cited Findings

**Microsoft 365 — tier ladder + add-ons + commitment, upgrade by step-up SKU**
- Step-up is the sanctioned upgrade mechanism between editions (M365 E3 → E5) and is a distinct SKU class excluded from EPT — [Microsoft Learn, OLS lifecycle and EPT](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/volume-licensing-central/learning/contracting/ols-lifecycle-and-ept); [View VL contracts](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/commerce/licenses/view-vl-contracts?view=o365-worldwide).
- Microsoft productizes add-ons as separately transactable SKUs attached to a base plan: "New Product Availability: Advanced Security Add-ons for Microsoft 365 Business Premium ... the new SKUs are now available and transactable in partner center" — [Microsoft Learn, Jan 2026](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-january).
- Bundling is done at the offer level, e.g. "Microsoft 365 + Copilot bundle", with partners asked to "target upcoming renewals for Microsoft 365 + Copilot bundle" — [same](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-january).
- Pricing catalog structure: license-based services have a monthly-refreshed price list **plus an "offer list matrix" that includes market availability**, with both list price and estimated retail price (ERP) per currency — [Microsoft Learn, Pricing and offers](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/pricing/pricing-and-offers).
- Annual billing is derived, not stored separately: "For annual billing frequency, multiply the monthly price by 12" — [same](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/pricing/pricing-and-offers).
- Real-world normalization: CDW sells "Microsoft 365 A3 - subscription license - 1 user" with chips `academic / hosted / EES / All Languages` and Mfg part `AAD-38391-12mo` — showing that segment (academic), hosting model, program (EES) and term (12mo) are all separate attributes — [CDW PDP](https://www.cdw.com/product/microsoft-365-a3-subscription-license-1-user/5419407).

**Adobe — segment-first navigation, app-bundle axis, co-termination**
- Adobe's plan page is segmented by audience first: `Individuals | Business | Students & Teachers | Schools & Universities`, and within Business by company size ("1 – 10 licenses" vs "Medium & large businesses") — [Adobe CC plans](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/plans.html); [Adobe CC for business](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/business/teams/plans.html).
- The product axis inside a segment is **app scope**: `Creative Cloud Single App for teams` (any one app) vs `Creative Cloud Pro` ("more than 20 apps including Photoshop, Illustrator, and Acrobat Pro") vs `Acrobat Single App` — [Adobe CC for business](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/business/teams/plans.html).
- Merchandising devices on the plan page: a "Most popular" badge on one card, and use-case tabs (`Photo`, `Graphic design`, `Illustration`, `Video`, `Social media`, `Acrobat & PDF`) that re-sort the same plan set — [same](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/business/teams/plans.html).
- Business vs individual is a genuine feature delta, not just pricing: teams adds admin console, 1TB pooled storage per user, 180-day version history, company-owned libraries, asset reclaim on departure, centralized IT deployment, 24x7 support — [Adobe, upgrade to teams](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/business/teams/upgradetoteams.html).
- Licensing program migration is itself a catalog event: legacy Adobe VIP was retired in favour of VIP Marketplace (deadline reported as 31 May 2025), with different renewal and cancellation mechanics — [Licenseware summary](https://licenseware.io/adobe-announces-retirement-of-vip-commercial-program-transition-to-vip-marketplace-by-may-31-2025/) (secondary source; treat the date as reported, not primary).
- VIP Marketplace operational constants that a catalog must encode: 14-day return window per licence, 3-day change/cancel cut-off before anniversary, co-termination of all team licences — [SoftwareOne docs](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/extensions/adobe-vip-marketplace/returns-and-termination/terminate-and-return-adobe-licenses.md); [Adobe subscription terms](https://www.adobe.com/legal/subscription-terms.html).

**Kaspersky — a single tier ladder with a feature-checklist comparison**
- Kaspersky Next is presented as "three online security options across two tiers" with a ladder: `EDR Foundations → EDR Optimum → XDR Optimum → MXDR Optimum`, each marked "Available for online purchase" where self-serve is possible — [Kaspersky SMB security](https://www.kaspersky.com/small-to-medium-business-security).
- The comparison is a **cumulative feature checklist**, not a spec table: Foundations lists endpoint protection, anti-ransomware, network security, cloud discovery & protection, task optimisation, root cause analysis; Optimum adds "Essential EDR Capabilities", "Cybersecurity Training for IT", "Microsoft Office 365 Security" — [same](https://www.kaspersky.com/small-to-medium-business-security).
- Kaspersky also ships the comparison as a downloadable feature-table PDF, i.e. the matrix is a maintained asset separate from the page — [Kaspersky Next feature table PDF](https://content.kaspersky-labs.com/fm/site-editor/0b/0bf633fef24479ec17e2075feaffa88d/source/kaspersky-next-optimum-feature-table.pdf).
- Entry-level framing is by business size and self-serve threshold ("Scalable protection for small and mid-sized businesses ... activate it online right away") — [Kaspersky SMB security](https://www.kaspersky.com/small-to-medium-business-security).

**КриптоПро — line × version × execution × license type, with version-comparison as a first-class page**
- The product tree is explicitly two-level in navigation: `КриптоПро CSP` has children `CSP Lite`, `TLS c ГОСТ`, `Java CSP`, `Winlogon`, `Считыватели`, `История версий`, `Сравнение версий` — i.e. the family has *sub-products* and *meta-pages* (history, comparison) as siblings — [КриптоПро CSP](https://cryptopro.ru/products/csp).
- "Сравнение версий КриптоПро CSP" is a matrix whose columns are **versions** (`CSP 4.0 R4 | CSP 5.0 | CSP 5.0 R2 | CSP 5.0 R3`) and whose row blocks are **supported OS/platform+arch**, **supported algorithms** (ГОСТ Р 34.10-2012, 34.11-2012, 34.12-2015, 34.13-2015, 28147-89), and **supported key media** (П / А / PKCS / ФКН) — [КриптоПро, сравнение версий](https://cryptopro.ru/products/csp/compare).
- Version granularity goes down to build level: a footnote keys a capability to "КриптоПро CSP 5.0 R3+ (сборка 5.0.13003)" — [same](https://cryptopro.ru/products/csp/compare).
- Old versions are retained as a distinct catalog section ("Продукты старых версий") with an upgrade nudge: "Все преимущества продуктов этих линеек не только сохраняются, но и приумножаются в КриптоПро CSP 5.0" — [КриптоПро CSP](https://cryptopro.ru/products/csp).
- Comparison pages for superseded versions are **archived rather than deleted** (2.0/3.0 and 3.6/3.9 comparisons exist only via Internet Archive links) — [КриптоПро, сравнение версий](https://cryptopro.ru/products/csp/compare).
- The order/SKU tree encodes the variant axes explicitly: `CSP 5.0`, `CSP 5.0 (для иностранных лиц)`, `CSP 4.0`, `CSP 4.0 Lic (для иностранных лиц)`, `CSP 5.0 КС3 Astra Linux Special Edition`, `CSP TLS` — [КриптоПро, сравнение версий (order links)](https://cryptopro.ru/products/csp/compare).
- For the УЦ product the SKU tree separates **base licence / extension / upgrade**: `Базовые лицензии ПАК "КриптоПро УЦ" 2.0 КС2`, `Расширение ПАК ... КС2`, `Обновление до ПАК ... КС2`, plus `Средства мониторинга УЦ` and a `Восстановление лицензии/сертификата` SKU — [КриптоПро, продукты (order menu)](https://cryptopro.ru/products/csp).

**МагПро / КриптоКом — Russian-market SKU naming carries edition, version, execution, term**
- Softline's PDP spec block for МагПро is the clearest public example of Russian B2B licence attributes: `Версия`, `Способ доставки (Физический)`, `Тип лицензии (Обновление)`, `Срок действия (бессрочная лицензия)`, `Тип организации (Коммерческая)` — [Softline store, МагПро КриптоПакет обновление](https://store.softline.ru/cryptocom/-525362/).

- Russian retail catalogs also expose `Артикул` and `НДС` as first-class PDP attributes alongside licence type — [Syssoft, МагПро](https://www.syssoft.ru/cryptocom/skzi-magpro-kriptopaket-utilita-openssl/cryp7747477/).

- The dominant navigation is **vendor landing page → family → variant list**, not a faceted search: Softline's menu has a dedicated `Программы и лицензии Microsoft Corporation` entry and per-vendor hubs (`/microsoft/`, `/kaspersky/`, `/drweb/`) — [Softline store](https://store.softline.ru/); [Softline B2B](https://store.softline.ru/b2b/).
- Softline's top-level taxonomy mixes product type with market intent — `Операционные системы и офисное ПО`, `Антивирусы и безопасность`, `САПР и ГИС`, `Графика, дизайн, мультимедиа`, `Управление бизнесом и финансами`, `Программирование и разработка`, **`Импортозамещение`**, `Образование и развлечения` — the last two being *cross-cutting* collections rather than product types — [Softline store](https://store.softline.ru/).
- `Импортозамещение` also appears as a leaf collection inside unrelated branches (e.g. `Российская коммуникационная платформа (Импортозамещение)` under internet/comms), confirming it is a **tag/collection**, not a tree node — [Softline store](https://store.softline.ru/cryptocom/-525362/).

### Inferences
- The correct abstraction is a **family with a declared "variation axis set"**, e.g. Microsoft: `{plan tier, add-on set, commitment}`; Adobe: `{segment, app scope, seat model}`; Kaspersky: `{protection tier, managed?, platform}`; КриптоПро: `{line, major.minor.build, execution, platform, license type}`; Dr.Web: `{suite, platform, licensing unit, term}`. One global "edition" enum will fail on all five.
- КриптоПро's treatment of `Обновление до ...` / `Расширение` / `Базовые лицензии` as *sibling SKUs of the same product* is the cleanest model for Russian SKZI vendors, and maps directly to relation types `upgrade_from`, `extends`, `base`.
- Encoding from-version/to-version inside the МагПро upgrade SKU name is a warning: without structured `upgrade_from_version` / `upgrade_to_version` fields, upgrade eligibility cannot be computed and must be read by a human.
- Archiving old comparison pages (КриптоПро) rather than deleting them is important for B2B: buyers still hold licences for 4.0 and need the matrix.

### Gaps
- No public Kaspersky page retrieved that documents the *legacy* business line (Small Office Security / Endpoint Security for Business Select-Advanced-Total) tiering as of 2026; only the Kaspersky Next ladder was verifiable.
- No public source confirms how КриптоПро assigns the `КС1/КС2/КС3` class labels (security-class semantics) beyond their use as SKU discriminators.
- Could not retrieve a public page confirming whether МагПро licences are upgradable across `исполнение` (e.g. OpenVPN-ГОСТ → КриптоТуннель) or only across versions.

## What is manual merchandising vs algorithmic recommendation? Any public evidence of rules or recommendations?

### Takeaway
Public evidence shows a clear split: **catalog structure, families, compare matrices and bundle composition are manually curated** (vendors define products; distributors define listings and categories), while **"what to sell to this customer next" is increasingly algorithmic and renewal-triggered** (Pax8 Opportunity Explorer, Microsoft Partner Center AI assistant). Nobody publishes the recommendation rules; the observable rules are *commercial* (eligibility, market availability, minimum quantity, self-serve thresholds, co-termination) rather than collaborative-filtering.

### Cited Findings
- **Curatorial responsibility sits with the vendor, not the marketplace.** SoftwareOne: products are "defined by vendors" and made orderable via listings; a vendor account is "used by vendors to define the product structure and configuration, and make those products available for ordering through the Marketplace" — [SoftwareOne docs, Key concepts](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/marketplace-platform/platform-overview/key-concepts.md).
- **Availability is rule-based, not editorial.** Microsoft's offer list matrix carries market availability per offer; private-preview and other-channel products are excluded from CSP price lists — [Microsoft Learn, Pricing and offers](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/pricing/pricing-and-offers).
- **Combination rules are enforced programmatically.** SoftwareOne product settings include "Activate item selection validation — used in the 'Purchase wizard' to validate vendor-specific compatibility of selected items, before the order is submitted" — [SoftwareOne Catalog API, Product](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/product.md).
- **Eligibility is a first-class listing attribute** (`eligibility.client`, `eligibility.partner`), meaning the same product can be offered to direct clients and withheld from partners — [SoftwareOne Catalog API, Listing](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/listing.md).
- **Pax8 is explicitly algorithmic on the "next product" question.** Opportunity Explorer "uses machine learning and trend analysis across our ecosystem of data"; the stated benefit is "Upsell/cross-sell/add-ons: Using trend analysis and comparisons to similar businesses, partners can quickly discover products to complement their existing services and solutions" — [Pax8 blog](https://www.pax8.com/blog/new-pax8-marketplace/).
- **Microsoft's recommendation engine is renewal-anchored and promotion-aware.** Partner Center AI assistant can "quickly identify upcoming Microsoft 365 Business renewals, recommended Copilot Business upsell paths, and eligible promotions"; it will "Recommend which Copilot Business bundle offer to upgrade to" and "Recommend more than one upgrade path with applicable promotions"; a documented zero-prompt is "Show customers renewing Microsoft 365 Business in the next 60 days and highlight which Copilot Business bundle to upgrade to" — [Microsoft Learn, Jan 2026](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-january).
- **The same assistant also answers upgrade-path and promotion-eligibility questions on a specific cart** — "available subscription upgrade paths", "promotion eligibility for orders and customers", "legacy to new commerce migration" — [Microsoft Learn, AI assistant in Customers workspace](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/customers/ai-assistant-customers-overview).
- **Promotions are attached to the recommendation, with explicit eligibility conditions.** Example: Purview Suite (formerly E5 Compliance) at 50% for accounts with existing or new M365 Copilot licences, prerequisite licensing required, "net-new Purview Suite license additions", extended to 30 June 2026 — [Microsoft Learn, Jan 2026](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-january).
- **Merchandising labels are manual.** Adobe's "Most popular" badge and the use-case tab ordering on the plans page are editorial placements, not computed — [Adobe CC for business](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/business/teams/plans.html).
- **Pax8 separates the two concerns by product surface**: Catalog = browse/compare/filter; Solutions Builder = human-authored packages ("Package multi-product offerings once, then sell them across your entire base"); Opportunity Explorer = algorithmic targeting — [Pax8 Marketplace tools](https://www.pax8.com/en-us/marketplace/tools/).
- **Generic commerce platforms expose exactly this split as configuration**: rule-based "Related Products / Up-Sells / Cross-Sells" attributes vs behavioural "Customers Also Bought" / "Who Bought This Also Bought" — [Magento/Aheadworks automatic related products](https://aheadworks.com/kbase/extension-automatic-related-products-2/); [Aheadworks who-bought-this-also-bought](https://aheadworks.com/kbase/extension-who-bought-this-also-bought/).

### Inferences
- A realistic 2026 architecture has **three recommendation layers**: (1) *deterministic* — family/variant adjacency, upgrade path, renewal due, eligibility, co-termination, minimum quantity; (2) *curated* — merchandiser-authored bundles, "better together" sets, migration campaigns; (3) *statistical* — "partners like you bought", renewal-window targeting. Layer (1) must exist first because it is the only one that can be *explained* to a B2B buyer and audited.
- The strongest public signal for B2B cross-sell is **renewal-date + adjacent SKU + eligible promotion**, not purchase correlation. That is a rule a catalog can implement without any ML.
- Because Microsoft and Pax8 both put recommendations behind an assistant/explorer rather than a PDP carousel, the recommended UI is a **"next best actions" worklist for the account manager**, plus modest on-PDP "complete your configuration" blocks.

### Gaps
- No public documentation of Pax8's or Microsoft's actual recommendation features, weights or thresholds; both describe capability, not mechanism.
- No public evidence of collaborative-filtering ("customers also bought") being used by any of the named software distributors; their public surfaces emphasise compare, configure and renew.
- No public source on how any distributor measures recommendation performance in B2B software.

## What can we borrow for relation types, family taxonomy, filtering and B2B buyer UX?

### Takeaway
Borrow four things: (1) the **Product/Item/Listing** separation from SoftwareOne, (2) the **attribute-grammar title** from SHI/CDW, (3) the **cumulative-tier compare matrix** from Kaspersky/Adobe/КриптоПро, and (4) the **lifecycle action model** (renew / step-up / migrate / co-terminate) from Microsoft and Adobe. Add Russian-market specifics as first-class attributes: `исполнение`, `тип лицензии` (новая/продление/обновление/расширение), `срок действия` (бессрочная/N мес.), `тип организации`, `минимальная покупка`, `сертификация (ФСТЭК/ФСБ/Минобороны)`, `реестр российского ПО`.

### Cited Findings
- **Product ≠ SKU ≠ offer.** Product is a curated group; Item is the transactable line; Listing binds product to seller + price list + eligibility — [SoftwareOne Product](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/product.md); [Item](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/items.md); [Listing](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/listing.md).
- **Terms as data, not text**: `{period, commitment}` lets one SKU express monthly billing on an annual commitment — [SoftwareOne Item](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/items.md).
- **Wizard groups with `required` / `multiple` / `displayOrder` / `default`** are the mechanism for "configure your licence" steps (choose edition → choose add-ons → choose term) — [SoftwareOne Item Group](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/item-group.md).
- **Vendor-specific validation hooks before order submission** are the mechanism for preventing invalid configurations (e.g. step-up only from the edition below; add-on requires base plan) — [SoftwareOne Product settings](https://docs.platform.softwareone.com/developer-resources/rest-api/catalog-api/product.md).
- **Attribute-grammar naming** proven at scale by two distributors: `<Product> - (v. N) - <license model> - <qty> <unit> - <volume tier> - <delivery> - <platform>` — [SHI](https://www.shi.com/product/48038154/Beyond-Compare-5-Pro-Edition-per-seat-100-Users); [CDW](https://www.cdw.com/category/software/?w=F).
- **License-type facets** buyers actually filter on, as published by CDW: site licence, software assurance, step-up, subscription licence renewal, subscription upgrade licence, product upgrade licence, subscription plan licence key, professional licence, and their `(renewal)` variants — [CDW](https://www.cdw.com/category/software/?w=F); [CDW.ca](https://www.cdw.ca/category/software/?w=F).
- **Price-band and rating facets** coexist with brand facets on the same listing page — [CDW Business Applications](https://www.cdw.com/category/software/business-applications/?w=FE).
- **Cumulative tier comparison** (Kaspersky) lets a buyer see "what do I gain by moving up one tier" without reading a full spec table — [Kaspersky SMB security](https://www.kaspersky.com/small-to-medium-business-security).
- **Version-matrix comparison** (КриптоПро) proves that for infrastructure software the *version × platform × algorithm* matrix is the primary buying tool, more than the edition matrix — [КриптоПро, сравнение версий](https://cryptopro.ru/products/csp/compare).
- **Licensing-unit documentation** (Dr.Web) shows that unit of measure is a buyer-facing decision ("by workstation / by terminal-server connection / by user / by device"), not back-office metadata — [Dr.Web](https://license.drweb.ru/products/biz/).
- **Self-serve threshold routing** — "buy online up to 250 workstations", "from partners: any quantity" — is a UX rule worth copying for B2B/B2C blending — [Dr.Web](https://license.drweb.ru/products/biz/).
- **Minimum purchase quantity as a displayed attribute** — "от 101 до 300 шт." — prevents dead-end carts — [Softline store](https://store.softline.ru/cryptocom/-525362/).
- **Bundle membership shown on the component page** ("also supplied as part of kits") is cheap to implement and high-value for SMB kits — [Dr.Web](https://license.drweb.ru/products/biz/).
- **Migration/alternative as a campaign with incentive** ("migrate with 50% discount") is the workable B2B form of "alternatives" — [Dr.Web migration promo](https://promotions.drweb.ru/promo/migrate/v2/).
- **PIM guidance on family/variant modelling**: define families, sellable items, variants, multipacks, bundles, accessories, replacement parts; use one `item_group_id` per family and a `variant_option` for the distinguishing attribute; never collapse several sellable variants into one record — [Catalog AI, PIM implementation](https://www.getcatalog.ai/blog/pim-implementation).
- **Variant acceptance test** worth adopting verbatim: "Add or change one size or color and confirm the correct offer publishes without changing its siblings" — [same](https://www.getcatalog.ai/blog/pim-implementation).
- **Russian-market certification dimension** is buyer-critical and must be filterable: Dr.Web exposes "Поставка версий Dr.Web, сертифицированных Минобороны России, ФСБ России, ФСТЭК России" as a distinct supply option — [Dr.Web](https://license.drweb.ru/products/biz/).
- **Registry membership** is a marketing/compliance attribute in Russian catalogs: "СКЗИ «МагПро КриптоПакет» включено в реестр российского ПО" — [Softline store](https://store.softline.ru/cryptocom/-525362/).
- **B2B-only guardrails** are stated on the PDP in Russian retail: "Доступно только юридическим лицам и ИП — не предназначено для личных, семейных, домашних и иных нужд" — [Softline store](https://store.softline.ru/cryptocom/-525362/).
- **Enterprise agreement as an upsell tier** is merchandised as a product: "Продукт Softline Enterprise Agreement оптимален для организаций от 300 пользователей (устройств)" — [Softline B2B](https://store.softline.ru/b2b/).
- **Assisted-selling entry points** sit beside the catalog: "Заявка на подбор товара" (product selection request) and "Бесплатный подбор решений от экспертов" — [Softline B2B](https://store.softline.ru/b2b/).
- **Pax8's Solutions Builder** is the reference for reusable multi-vendor packages sold as one line — [Pax8 Marketplace tools](https://www.pax8.com/en-us/marketplace/tools/).
- **Upgrade pricing can be a published percentage rule.** МагПро's "Замена СКЗИ ... в.3.0 ... на ... в.4.0" SKUs are priced at exactly 50% of the corresponding new licence (42 000→21 000; 1 700→850; 6 400→3 200; 2 050→1 025) — [Криптоком, прайс-лист 20.01.2026](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- **Volume ladder as an item-level price table, with an intentional no-price top band**: `От 1 до 10` (0%), `От 10 до 30` (−3%), `От 30 до 100` (−5%), `От 100 до 300` (−10%), `От 300` → "по запросу" — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- **"Price on request" must be a state, not a null.** The same price list also routes entire SKUs away from the vendor ("Для приобретения просьба обращаться в компанию «Нума Технологии»"), so fulfilment routing is per-SKU — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- **VAT varies per price line, not per catalog**: licence lines are "Не облагается НДС ... подпункт 26 пункта 2 статьи 149 НК РФ" while the support line includes "НДС 5%" — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- **Variant attribute can carry the certification class**: МагПро `исполнение` 3/5/7 → КС1, 4/6/8 → КС2, each with its own ФСБ certificate number and date — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).

### Inferences — proposed relation set for a Softline-like catalog

The following is a **design proposal derived from the cited patterns**, not a description of any single vendor's schema.

| Relation | Direction | Cardinality | Source pattern | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `family_contains_variant` | family → item | 1:N | SoftwareOne Product→Item; PIM `item_group_id` | Family page lists all SKUs |
| `variant_of` (inverse) | item → family | N:1 | SHI/CDW title grammar | Breadcrumb + canonical URL |
| `edition_step_up` | item → item (higher tier) | N:M, acyclic | Microsoft Step-Up; M365 E3→E5 | "Upgrade to" CTA with price delta |
| `edition_step_down` | item → item | N:M | inferred from step-up inverse | Downgrade at renewal |
| `addon_requires_base` | item → item | N:M | SoftwareOne `itemSelection` validation; MS add-on SKUs | Prevent invalid carts |
| `addon_compatible_with` | item → item | N:M | Dr.Web base + components matrix | Add-on suggestions |
| `component_of_suite` | item → item | N:M | Dr.Web «Варианты лицензий» | Show what's included |
| `bundle_contains` | bundle → item | 1:N | Pax8 Solutions Builder; CDW Better Together; Dr.Web kits | Kit PDP + component "also in kits" |
| `renewal_of` | renewal SKU → original SKU | N:1 | CDW `Subscription License Renewal`; КриптоПро `Обновление` | Renewal cart from subscription |
| `upgrade_from_version` / `upgrade_to_version` | upgrade SKU → version | N:1 each | МагПро 3.0→4.0; КриптоПро 4.0→5.0 | Eligibility check |
| `upgrade_price_rule` | upgrade SKU → base SKU | N:1 | МагПро «Замена» at 50% of list | Price the upgrade automatically |
| `extends_capacity` | extension SKU → base SKU | N:1 | КриптоПро `Расширение ПАК УЦ` | Grow without re-buying |
| `migration_from` (competitor/legacy program) | item → item/program | N:M | Dr.Web 50% migration; MS EA→CSP | Switch campaign landing |
| `supersedes` | item → item | 1:N | КриптоПро «Продукты старых версий» | Hide from search, keep buyable |
| `alternative_in_category` | item → item | N:M | (curated) | "Alternatives" block, human-set |
| `same_family_different_segment` | item → item | N:M | Adobe individual vs teams; CDW `academic` | Cross-segment upsell |
| `compatible_platform` | item → platform enum | N:M | КриптоПро OS matrix; Dr.Web platform variants | Filter + compatibility check |
| `certified_by` | item → certification | N:M | Dr.Web ФСТЭК/ФСБ/МО; реестр ПО | Compliance filter |
| `requires_key_media` | item → media type | N:M | КриптоПро П/А/PKCS/ФКН | Hardware accessory cross-sell |
| `fulfilled_by_partner` | item → partner | N:M | МагПро Numa Edge → «Нума Технологии» | Route non-stocked SKUs |

Item/offer-level attributes the sources justify (not relations, but needed alongside them): `volume_price_tiers[]` (`1–10 / 10–30 / 30–100 / 100–300 / 300+`), `price_on_request` flag, `vat_treatment` per price line, `delivery_method` per listing (electronic / physical CD + формуляр), `min_purchase_qty`, `self_serve_max_qty`, `certificate{number, body, valid_from, valid_to}`, `registry_entry`, `platform_mode` (per-SKU vs order-parameter).

Additional inferences:
- **Taxonomy should be faceted, not a single tree.** Softline's `Импортозамещение` behaves as a collection across branches; CDW's licence-type facets cut across categories. Model: one shallow browse tree (≤3 levels) + orthogonal facet groups (vendor, licence type, term, unit of measure, platform, segment, certification, delivery, language).
- **Title should be rendered from attributes** using the SHI/CDW grammar, with a manual `display_name` override only for marketing surfaces.
- **The "compare" unit should be the family, not the SKU.** Comparing 40 МагПро SKUs is useless; comparing 4 КриптоПро versions × platforms is useful. Default compare columns = the family's declared variation axis (version for КриптоПро, tier for Kaspersky, plan for M365).
- **B2B UX needs three entry modes**, all observed publicly: (a) *I know the SKU* → search by vendor part number (CDW shows `Mfg #` next to `CDW #`); (b) *I know the product* → family page with variant matrix; (c) *I know the need* → category/collection + "подбор" assisted request.
- **Show lifecycle state on the item, not just availability**: `новая / продление / обновление / расширение / восстановление` — КриптоПро even sells `Восстановление лицензии/сертификата`, which most catalogs omit and which generates real support load.
- **Co-termination must be visible before purchase.** Adobe's "all team licences co-terminate" is the single most consequential B2B billing rule; a catalog that does not show the resulting anniversary date will produce renewal disputes.

### Gaps
- No public source describes a distributor's actual relation-type schema beyond SoftwareOne's Catalog API; the table above is therefore a synthesis, and field names are proposals.
- No public evidence on how any distributor handles *downgrade* SKUs or proration rules for mid-term edition changes (Microsoft documents step-up but not step-down mechanics publicly).
- Could not verify whether Softline's public storefront models `обновление` as a relation to a source SKU or as an independent product with a descriptive name; the PDP attributes suggest the latter, which is the weaker design.
- No public data on Russian-market catalog size, SKU counts, or family counts for КриптоПро/Dr.Web/МагПро resellers.

### Russian-market specifics: КриптоПро, МагПро, Dr.Web alongside global vendors

#### Takeaway
Russian software catalogs add **four dimensions that global distributor schemas lack**: certification body (ФСТЭК/ФСБ/Минобороны), registry membership (реестр российского ПО), `исполнение` (a numerically coded build/task variant of the same crypto product, which simultaneously determines the FSB security class), and a much richer licence-type vocabulary (новая / продление / обновление / замена / расширение / восстановление). These must be first-class facets, not description text, or the catalog cannot answer the dominant Russian B2B query: "what do I need to buy to make my current licence valid/compliant/upgraded?" The МагПро vendor price list additionally shows upgrade pricing published as a fixed percentage and volume discounts as a table with a deliberate "по запросу" top band.

#### Cited Findings
- КриптоПро sells `Восстановление лицензии/сертификата ТП/бухгалтерских документов` as its own order category — a licence-recovery SKU with no global equivalent — [КриптоПро, продукты](https://cryptopro.ru/products/csp).
- КриптоПро product lines are themselves a family tree with sub-brands sharing a name stem: `CSP`, `CSP Lite`, `CSP TLS`, `Java CSP`, `JCP`, `JTLS`, `.NET`, `HSM`, `Ключ`, `Ключ Lite`, `DSS`, `NGate`, `УЦ` — [КриптоПро, продукты](https://cryptopro.ru/products/csp).
- `NGate` is further split by gateway function: `VPN-шлюз`, `TLS-шлюз`, `Шлюз портального доступа`, `IPsec VPN-шлюз` — an "edition" axis that is really a *deployment role* axis — [КриптоПро, продукты](https://cryptopro.ru/products/csp).
- Dr.Web exposes certification-specific supply as a distinct path rather than an attribute: "Поставка версий Dr.Web, сертифицированных Минобороны России, ФСБ России, ФСТЭК России" — [Dr.Web](https://license.drweb.ru/products/biz/).
- Dr.Web also maintains `Лицензии и сертификаты` and `Продукты Dr.Web в реестре отечественного ПО` as separate navigational destinations — [Dr.Web, продукты для профессионального использования](https://products.drweb.ru/).
- Russian retail PDPs standardise a licence-attribute block (`Тип лицензии`, `Срок действия`, `Тип организации`, `Платформа`, `Версия`, `Способ доставки`, `Минимальная покупка`, `Артикул`, `НДС`) that is remarkably consistent across resellers — [Softline store](https://store.softline.ru/cryptocom/-525362/); [Syssoft](https://www.syssoft.ru/cryptocom/skzi-magpro-kriptopaket-utilita-openssl/cryp7747477/); [Kvantech](https://kvan.tech/catalog/programmnoe-obespechenie/antivirusi-bezopasnost/bezopasnost-zaschita-informatsii/kriptokom-magpro-kriptopaket-obnovlenie-skzi-magpro-kriptopaket-v-3-0-ispolnenie-openvpn-gost-litsen/).
- Physical delivery remains a real fulfilment mode for Russian SKZI: Softline lists `Способ доставки: Физический` on the PDP — [Softline store](https://store.softline.ru/cryptocom/-525362/) — and a reseller states the CD-with-формуляр cost explicitly: "Стоимость дистрибутива на компакт-диске с формуляром - 600 рублей, включена в лицензию" — [Secure-market, МагПро](https://www.secure-market.ru/product/magpro-kriptopaket).
- Softline merchandises `Импортозамещение` as a standing collection at the top of the catalog, alongside product-type categories — [Softline store](https://store.softline.ru/).
- Softline's B2B page positions a domestic enterprise agreement tier (300+ users) and assisted selection ("Заявка на подбор товара", "Бесплатный подбор решений от экспертов") — [Softline B2B](https://store.softline.ru/b2b/).

**МагПро / Криптоком — the vendor's own price list is the best public specimen of a Russian SKZI variant model**

The vendor publishes a dated price list (20 January 2026) that exposes the full variant grammar that resellers then flatten into product names:

- The product is declared as one product delivered "в различных **исполнениях**, нацеленных на решение различных прикладных задач" — so `исполнение` is the vendor's own top-level variant axis, not a reseller invention — [Криптоком, прайс-лист, 20.01.2026](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- `исполнение` is **numerically coded and paired with a security class**: "Утилита openssl" = исполнение 3, 4; "КриптоТуннель" = исполнение 5, 6; "OpenVPN-ГОСТ" = исполнение 7, 8 — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- The odd/even split is the certification class: КС1 covers исполнения 3, 5, 7 (сертификат ФСБ РФ № СФ/114-5043 от 17.12.2024); КС2 covers исполнения 4, 6, 8 (№ СФ/124-5044 от 17.12.2024) — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf). **One variant attribute simultaneously determines the application task and the certificate**, which is why the certificate cannot be a free-text field.
- Licence-type axis inside each исполнение: `серверная лицензия`, `клиентская лицензия`, `лицензия для генерации сертификатов`, `лицензия для шлюза филиала`, `лицензия для отдельного компьютера`, `лицензия для использования в составе ПАК Numa Edge` — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- Term axis: `на неограниченный срок` vs `на 1 год`, priced separately for the same исполнение × licence type (e.g. серверная OpenSSL: 42 000 ₽ perpetual vs 16 800 ₽ for 1 year) — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- **Upgrade is a distinct "Замена" (replacement) SKU, priced at exactly 50% of the new licence**: 42 000 → 21 000; 1 700 → 850; 6 400 → 3 200; 2 050 → 1 025 — a published, computable upgrade rule rather than a negotiated discount — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- Quantity-break ladder is explicit and applied to the whole price list: `От 1 до 10` (0%), `От 10 до 30` (−3%), `От 30 до 100` (−5%), `От 100 до 300` (−10%), `От 300` = "по запросу" (quote only, no list price) — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- Technical support is a priced percentage, not a SKU family: "15% от стоимости неограниченных лицензий, но не менее 1050 рублей" per year — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- **Platform is an order parameter, not a SKU attribute**: "При оформлении заявки ... необходимо указать операционную систему из приведенного выше списка, а также требуемую разрядность (32 или 64 бита)" — the OS list is a fixed enumerated list (Windows/Debian/Ubuntu/RHEL/Astra/РЕД ОС/РОСА/Эльбрус-class distros/macOS/Solaris/OpenWRT) — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- Platform constrains which licence types exist: "Для Sun Solaris заказ лицензии для отдельного компьютера невозможен" — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- Physical media is mandatory by regulation and priced separately: "По требованиям ФСБ России поставка СКЗИ осуществляется на материальном носителе. Стоимость дистрибутива на компакт-диске с формуляром — 700 рублей" — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- Some SKUs in the same price list are **not sold by the vendor at all**: Numa Edge licences say "Для приобретения просьба обращаться в компанию «Нума Технологии»" — i.e. fulfilment routing is a per-SKU property — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- Tax treatment is mixed within one document and is a live data-quality hazard: licence prices are footnoted "Не облагается НДС в соответствии с подпунктом 26 пункта 2 статьи 149 Налогового кодекса РФ", while the support line states "в том числе и НДС 5%" — [same](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf).
- Price and packaging drift between vendor and reseller is observable: the vendor's Jan-2026 CD/формуляр price is 700 ₽, while a reseller page states 600 ₽ "включена в лицензию" — [Криптоком](https://cryptocom.ru/buy/cryptocom_pricelist.pdf) vs [Secure-market](https://www.secure-market.ru/product/magpro-kriptopaket).

#### Inferences
- A Russian-market catalog needs a `licence_lifecycle` enum distinct from the global one: `new | renewal | upgrade(version) | extension(capacity/term) | recovery | migration`. `recovery` and `extension` are the two that global schemas consistently lack.
- `исполнение` should be modelled as a variant attribute with a controlled vocabulary per vendor (МагПро: `КриптоТуннель`, `OpenVPN-ГОСТ`, `Утилита openssl`), because it changes both price and compatibility. The МагПро price list shows it should carry a **numeric code and a certification class** in the same record (`исполнение 5` → КС1), so a single variant row answers both "what does it do" and "what is it certified for".
- Certification should be a facet *and* a relation to a certificate record with validity dates, since Russian B2B buyers filter by "certified by ФСТЭК" and by certificate currency.
- Because delivery can be physical (CD + формуляр), the offer/listing layer must support `delivery_method` per listing, not per product — reinforcing the SoftwareOne Product/Listing split.
- МагПро's "Замена ... в.3.0 ... на ... в.4.0" at a fixed 50% of list price suggests Russian SKZI upgrade pricing is often a **published percentage rule**, so `upgrade_price_rule` should be a field on the upgrade relation, not a manually typed price. That also gives a cheap sanity check when a reseller's price disagrees.
- The quantity ladder (`1–10 / 10–30 / 30–100 / 100–300 / 300+ по запросу`) is a **volume-price table attached to the item**, and the top band deliberately has no price — so the catalog must support "price on request" as a first-class state rather than a null price.
- Platform-as-order-parameter (МагПро) is the opposite of platform-as-variant (Dr.Web, КриптоПро). Both are legitimate; the difference is whether the vendor certifies per-OS SKUs or certifies one SKU across an OS list. The catalog needs a per-family flag for this, or it will either explode SKU counts or lose compatibility data.
- Mixed VAT treatment inside one price list means VAT must be an attribute of the *price record* (per line), not a global setting.

#### Gaps
- No public source documents how Russian distributors model `исполнение` vs `версия` vs `редакция` in their internal PIM; only vendor price lists and storefront attributes were observable.
- Could not confirm whether certification validity dates are exposed as structured data anywhere in Russian catalogs (only certificate list pages and certificate-number text were reachable).
- No public source explains how resellers reconcile vendor price-list changes (the 600 ₽ vs 700 ₽ CD discrepancy) with already-published catalog prices.
- Could not determine whether КриптоПро's `КС1/КС2/КС3` labels and МагПро's `исполнение 3–8` numbering follow a shared industry convention or are vendor-specific; no normative document was located.
